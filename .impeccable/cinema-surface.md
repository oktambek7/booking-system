## Direction contract

THESIS
Choosing a film and a seat should feel like the house lights going down: one warm pool of light, one clear gold action, and a hall map you can read at a glance. It refuses the usual booking page, a dense grid of competing buttons.

OWN-WORLD
The Darkened Auditorium: near-black ink ground (#0B0D17) falling to deep ink (#07080F), a still gold glow above the screen, ivory type, dusk for everything quiet. Syne headings over Outfit body. One gold gradient is reserved for the primary action. Seats are dusk, gold, ivory and a hatched ink. A light theme (Ivory, #F1ECE2) shares every token and swaps in the deeper gold (#A8823D). Recognisable with all copy removed: the ticket mark with its single gold dot, and the glowing screen arc above the seat map.

STORY
A visitor opens the site on a phone, swipes through what is playing, picks a showtime from grouped time chips, chooses up to eight seats, sees the full summary and a ten-minute hold, confirms with an email code in a clearly labelled demo checkout, and keeps a QR ticket.

FIRST VIEWPORT
Desktop: sticky header (ticket mark and wordmark, nav, one account control), then a full-bleed hero tinted by the poster. An info card sits left (title, age badge, release and language, the next showtimes as chips, one gold "Get tickets" button and a secondary save), the poster stands right. Phone: poster first, then the card, controls in one row. The primary action is always the gold pill inside the card.

FORM
Direction A "Poster Glow": the real posters are the main colour, the chrome stays ink, ivory and gold, and few blurred layers keep it smooth on phones.

FINISH
Reviewed with axe-core (WCAG 2.1 AA) in dark and light at 1440px and 360px, then documented in DESIGN.md; brand assets come unchanged from the Phantora brand kit.
